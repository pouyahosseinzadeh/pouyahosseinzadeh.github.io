About

Computer science research with real-world stakes

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Utah State University. My work focuses on predictive models for space weather events and their role in protecting modern technical infrastructure.

I am especially interested in the intersection of machine learning, scientific data, and applied systems where forecasts can support better decisions.

Methods and tools

Machine learning, scientific computing, predictive modeling, and applied data analysis.

Research domains

Space weather, technical infrastructure, communications, defense, and space exploration.

Collaboration style

I value clear modeling assumptions, careful evaluation, and research communication that makes technical findings usable.
